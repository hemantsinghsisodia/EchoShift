THE ECHO

Everything you do is recorded. Every 15 seconds the recording freezes and an Echo of you appears where you stood when that cycle began, then repeats your exact movements and button presses.

When an Echo finishes its replay it holds its final position, keeping plates pressed for you.

Each room allows a limited number of Echoes. When a new one appears beyond the limit, the oldest one dissolves.
